# 🎯 Quests & Achievements

The Rewards Hub is your daily checklist. Everything here pays leaderboard points.

## Quests

| Quest | Type | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🩸 **First Buy** — make your first token buy | One-time | +5 |
| 🏗️ **Launch a Token** — create your first fusion | One-time | +20 |
| 🤝 **Bring a Friend** — refer a trader who buys $50+ | One-time | +25 |
| ⚡ **Daily Trader** — make at least one trade today | Daily | +3 |
| 📊 **Volume Runner** — trade $10 of volume today | Daily | +10 |

Daily quests reset at midnight UTC. Clearing both dailies every day of a week is +91 points before you've done anything else.

## Achievements (badges)

One-time badges that show on your profile — most pay points too:

| Badge | How to earn it | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🩸 **First Blood** | Complete your first trade | — |
| 🏗️ **Creator** | Launch a token on Fyuz | +10 |
| 🐋 **Whale** | Make a single trade over $100 | +15 |
| 🎓 **Graduate** | Hold a token through graduation | +15 |
| 🔥 **Week Warrior** | Reach a 7-day trading streak | +20 |

Achievements grant automatically the moment their condition is met — no claiming needed.

## Streaks

Trade at least once a day to build your streak. Your current and longest streaks show in the Rewards Hub — and a 7-day run unlocks Week Warrior.

{% hint style="info" %}
All quest and achievement points are **idempotent** — recorded once with a unique key, so nothing can double-grant, and nothing gets lost.
{% endhint %}
