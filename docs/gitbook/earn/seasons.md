# 🏁 Seasons

Seasons are Fyuz's grand tournaments — longer arcs layered on top of the weekly reward rounds, with a headline prize pot.

## Season 1

* A time-boxed competition with a live countdown on the **Season** page
* **Prize pot: 1 BNB** for the season's top performers
* Season score = your **net trading volume** during the season window **plus** every bonus point you earned in it (quests, referrals, achievements, creator bonuses)
* The **top 50** with positive scores make the season board

## How seasons differ from rounds

| | Weekly rounds | Seasons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | ~1 week | Weeks to months |
| Pot | 0.2% of all trade volume, continuously | Fixed headline prize |
| Winners | Top 100 + 1 lottery winner | Top 50 |
| Resets | Every distribution | Every season |

Both run at the same time and both feed off the same activity — a good week of trading and recruiting moves you up **both** boards at once.
