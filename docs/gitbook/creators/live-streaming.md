# 📺 Live Streaming

Every token page doubles as a stage. Go live, talk your book, build your cult following.

## For streamers

* Start a stream straight from your token's page — browser-native WebRTC, no OBS or stream keys required (though you can bring them).
* Your stream appears embedded on the token page with a live viewer count.
* Streams are powered by a dedicated LiveKit SFU — low-latency, real-time, built for conversations rather than 30-second-delay broadcasts.

## For viewers

* A 🔴 LIVE badge appears on streaming tokens across the board — one click to tune in.
* Viewer counts are real: heartbeat-based presence, not vanity numbers.
* Chat with the token's community on the same page while you watch.

## Why stream?

The tokens that graduate are the ones with a human behind them. A creator who shows up live — explaining the fusion, rallying holders, reacting to the chart — converts spectators into holders in a way no static page can.

{% hint style="info" %}
Streaming is available to token creators on their own token pages. Keep it legal and keep it about the token — streams that break the rules get cut.
{% endhint %}
