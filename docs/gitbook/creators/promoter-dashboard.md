# 📊 Promoter Dashboard

Launched some fusions? The Promoter Dashboard is your command center.

## Your numbers at a glance

| Stat | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Tokens created** | Every fusion you've launched |
| **Graduated to DEX** | How many made it to PancakeSwap — your championship count |
| **Trading volume** | Total volume across all your tokens |
| **Fees collected** | Your 0.2% cut of every trade on your tokens |

## Per-token cards

Each of your tokens shows its volume, fees, holders, unique traders, buy/sell counts, and — for tokens still on the curve — a live **graduation progress bar**. Graduated tokens wear the 🎓 badge.

## What creating pays

1. **0.2% of every trade** on your token, streamed to your wallet as it happens. A token that does $50K volume pays you $100 — even if you never touch it again.
2. **+50 leaderboard points** every time one of your tokens graduates.
3. **+20 points** for your first launch (quest) and the **Creator** badge (+10).

## Creator playbook

* **Fuse something memorable** — the image is the marketing. Weird pairings travel further than safe ones.
* **Make an opening buy** — it protects your launch from snipers and signals conviction.
* **Pick your battlefield** — choose the PancakeSwap version your community actually uses for the graduation pool.
* **Stream it** — token pages support [live streaming](live-streaming.md). A creator talking is worth a thousand chart candles.
