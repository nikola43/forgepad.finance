# 🧬 Create a Token

Creating a Fyuz token is not "upload a JPEG and pray." It's a fusion ritual.

## The flow

### 1. Name your two characters

Pick any two icons — real, fictional, mythical, absurd. The stranger the pairing, the more memorable the fusion. You have up to 200 characters to describe each one.

### 2. Pick a style (or spin the wheel)

Choose from 24+ curated art universes — Dragon Ball Z, Cyberpunk neon, Studio Ghibli, Pixel art, Film noir, Vaporwave, Baroque royal portrait, and more — or leave it blank and let Fyuz pick one at random. You can also type your own free-text style.

### 3. Name the coin

Token name + ticker. This is what shows on the board and on PancakeSwap after graduation.

### 4. Watch the fusion happen

The AI forges your two characters into **one original character** — a single portrait that becomes your token's permanent identity. Generation takes seconds; a progress bar keeps you posted, and there's a Retry button if you want another roll before committing.

### 5. Choose your launch

| Mode | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Bonding curve** (default) | Token launches on the curve with 1B supply. Trades immediately. Graduates to PancakeSwap at $30K market cap. |
| **Direct launch** | 1B supply straight to a PancakeSwap pool — no curve phase. For when you want a plain DEX token. |

You also pick which PancakeSwap version (V2 or V3) receives the liquidity at graduation.

### 6. (Optional but smart) Make an opening buy

Your creation transaction can include the **first buy** of your own token. This is sniper protection: bots watching for new launches can't front-run a curve where the creator already holds the floor.

## What creating earns you

* **Launch a Token** quest: +20 points
* **Creator** badge: +10 points
* **+50 points** for every token of yours that graduates
* **0.2% of every trade** on your token, forever — see [Fees](../how-it-works/fees.md)

{% hint style="warning" %}
Image generation is a paid operation, so it's gated by a wallet signature — you'll sign a message (free, no gas) before the fusion renders. One signature per generation.
{% endhint %}
