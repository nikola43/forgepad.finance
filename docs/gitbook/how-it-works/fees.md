# 💸 Fees

One fee. One percent. Three destinations. No hidden take.

## The 1% trade fee

Every buy and sell on a Fyuz bonding curve pays a **1% protocol fee**, split on-chain in the same transaction:

| Share | Goes to | What it funds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0.5%** | Treasury | Running the platform — infra, AI image generation, development |
| **0.3%** | **Rewards pot (Distributor)** | The BNB that gets paid back out to traders — see [Reward Distribution](../earn/reward-distribution.md) |
| **0.2%** | **The token's creator** | Passive income for launching a fusion that trades |

## What this means in practice

* **Traders:** just under a third of every fee you pay comes back to the community pot you can win from. Trade more, climb the leaderboard, get a bigger slice.
* **Creators:** launch a token that does $100K of volume and you've earned $200 in BNB without touching anything.
* **Everyone:** there are no deposit fees, no withdrawal fees, no "premium tiers." The 1% is the whole story.

{% hint style="info" %}
The pot is funded by the same volume it rewards, which is what makes wash-trading structurally unprofitable rather than something we have to police. $1 of self-dealt volume pays **1%** in fees and adds 0.3% to the pot, of which 90% is payable pro-rata — so even a farmer who *was* 100% of platform volume gets back at most **0.27%**. Farming loses ~3.7x, at any size.
{% endhint %}
